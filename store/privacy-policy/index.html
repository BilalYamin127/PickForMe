Privacy Policy

1. About this policy

PickForMe – Random Decision Maker is an offline application for everyday random choices. This policy explains how the current version handles information when you use its wheel, list picker, saved lists, history, settings and sharing feature.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the decision titles and options you enter, saved-list identifiers and timestamps, selected results and their timestamps and selection modes, and preferences such as theme, sound and vibration. This information is stored locally using Hive CE. No account or authentication is required.

3. Information we collect

The current app does not send your decision lists, results or preferences to the developer. It has no developer-operated backend, advertising SDK, analytics service, tracking technology or in-app purchase system. We do not collect device identifiers or location through the app. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

4. How your information is used

Local data is used to display and edit your lists, make random selections, show decision history, and remember your preferences. Random selection is performed on the device. Core decision-making features work offline.

5. Sharing a result

Sharing is optional and starts only when you tap the share action. The app gives the selected result text to your device’s native share sheet. You choose whether to share it and which app or person receives it. The receiving app or service processes that information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive a copy of shared results through a server.

6. Device services and backups

The app uses operating-system services for storage, sound, haptic feedback and the share sheet. Sound and vibration availability depend on your device and settings. Device-level backups or transfers may include app data depending on your operating system and backup settings. Those backups are controlled by you and your device or backup provider, not by a PickForMe server. If you use a browser preview, its hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as an IP address, to serve the preview; this policy’s offline-app statements describe the native mobile app.

7. Retention and deletion

Saved lists remain on your device until you delete them or clear app data. Deleting a saved list does not automatically delete its earlier history entries. You can clear decision history from History or Settings. To remove all locally stored lists, results and preferences, clear the app’s storage through your device settings or uninstall it; on some devices reinstallation or device restoration may restore a backup. Manage any backups through your device or backup-provider settings.

8. Security

Local app data is kept in the application’s storage area using the protections supplied by the operating system. The current app does not add its own database encryption or require an app passcode. Avoid entering sensitive information into decision titles or options. No method of storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children

PickForMe is a general-purpose decision tool and does not intentionally collect personal information from children or other users through the app. It does not include accounts, advertising or social profiles. A parent or guardian should supervise sharing through other apps where appropriate.

10. Contacting us

If you email support, your email provider and ours will process the address and information you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and do not use it for advertising. Do not include sensitive information in support messages. For privacy questions, contact Bilal Yamin at zulqarnain.tech9068@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and its last-updated date revised. Review the current policy before using a newly released version.
